About Sweet Crumbs Bakery

Sweet Crumbs is a South African small business established in 2021. The bakery
was created to provide freshly baked products for everyday customers and special occasions
its product range includes custom cakes,cupcakes,brownies,cookies and dessert boxes.

Our main goals are to:

  • Improve overall customer experience
  • Create custom cakes that match your vision
  • Keep improving so your next visit is even better
  • Deliver high quality baked goods that bring warmth
  • Deliver friendly service with every order

Our History

SC bakery was established in 2021 by a passionate baker(ODIRILE)
known as Mr O who wanted to create a bakery that combined traditional
baking with modern flavours. The bakery started as a small home based
business serving customers in local communiy.As demand increased, SC
expanded its product ranges.

Our Mission

Is to provide freshly baked, high quality products
that bring people together and make everyday moments and
special occasions more memorable

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted local bakery
known for quality products,creative designs and
and stellar customer service.
